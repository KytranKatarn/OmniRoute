Who is this site for?

This determines content priority, tone, and what sections we build. Pick your primary audience.

A

Potential Clients / Businesses

"I need AI solutions for my business." Focus on services, pricing, case studies, ROI. Professional tone, clear CTAs to book demos or sign up. SEO targets: AI consulting, SaaS platform, business automation.

B

Tech Community / Developers

"Wow, look what this platform can do." Focus on technical capability, architecture, agent roster, fleet nodes, open innovation. Impressed by the engineering. SEO targets: AI agents, multi-model orchestration, self-hosted AI.

C

Investors / Partners

"This is a serious platform with traction." Focus on scale, vision, team depth (107 agents), unique differentiation, roadmap. Authority and credibility. SEO targets: AI startup, enterprise AI platform.

D

All of the Above — Multi-Audience

Different sections target different audiences. Hero impresses everyone, then branches: Services for clients, Platform/Team for techies, Vision for investors. Each section pulls its weight for different visitors.
