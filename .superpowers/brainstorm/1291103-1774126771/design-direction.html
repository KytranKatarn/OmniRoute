A. Cyberpunk Aurora

Flowing aurora gradients, glassmorphism cards, glowing accents. Think: Vercel + Linear + Stripe merged with sci-fi energy. Deep purples, cyans, and golds against dark navy. Heavy on visual effects — GSAP scroll animations, parallax, magnetic cursors, particle backgrounds.

Pros

  • Maximum wow factor
  • Matches ARCHIE's sci-fi identity
  • Unique — no one else looks like this

Cons

  • Higher animation overhead
  • Complex to maintain
