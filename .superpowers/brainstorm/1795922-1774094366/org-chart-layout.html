C. Department Cards Grid

Leadership banner at top, then a responsive grid of department cards with member count + avatar thumbnails. Click to expand into full team list. Mobile-friendly, scannable at a glance, easy to find people.

My Recommendation: A (Classic Tree) or C (Cards Grid)

A matches the Lucidchart reference you showed — professional, familiar, great for showing reporting relationships. C is more modern and mobile-friendly — better for a public-facing page where visitors want to find someone quickly. B looks cool but gets messy with 16 departments.
