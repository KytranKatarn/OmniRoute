Quick Start Guide

  • 1Download a KawPow-compatible miner such as T-Rex, NBMiner, or lolMiner.
  • 2Configure your miner with the stratum URL above and your RVN wallet as the username.
  • 3Append your worker name after a dot: WALLET.rig1
  • 4Start mining and check your stats in the My Miner tab.
  • 5Payouts are automatic once your balance reaches the minimum threshold.

Example Config (T-Rex)

t-rex -a kawpow \
  -o stratum+tcp://pool.kytranempowerment.com:3333 \
  -u YOUR_RVN_WALLET.worker1 \
  -p x
