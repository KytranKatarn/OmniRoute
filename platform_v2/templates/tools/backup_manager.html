RETENTION POLICY

Retention Policy — automatic, per-component & per-tier

Backups are pruned automatically every night by the platform's single retention engine (scripts/backup/retention_prune.py, run by backup_triage.sh). It keeps a Grandfather-Father-Son set with a different daily / weekly / monthly count for every component and storage tier — for example the database keeps 30 daily + 12 weekly + 6 monthly on the primary disk, but only 2 daily on Google Drive. There is no single global number to set, and nothing to prune by hand: the nightly engine is the only retention path.

W.A.R.D.E.N. Pre-Deploy Governance Gates
