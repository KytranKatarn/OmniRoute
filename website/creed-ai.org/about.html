About the Institute

Mission

C.R.E.E.D. — the Compliance, Rights & Ethical Enforcement Directive — is an independent AI ethics governance institute. We develop preventive ethics frameworks for artificial intelligence before autonomous systems create harm that cannot be reversed.

Vision

A world where artificial agents are governed by robust, transparent, and humane ethical frameworks — established proactively, not reactively.

Advisory Board

Positions Open

We seek researchers, ethicists, policymakers, and industry leaders committed to preventive AI governance. If you believe ethics should precede capability, we want to hear from you.

Academic Partnership

C.R.E.E.D. is structured for academic partnership. We welcome collaboration with SSHRC, ISED, and Quebec innovation programs. Our research programs are designed to produce peer-reviewable, grant-eligible outputs that advance the field of preventive AI ethics.
