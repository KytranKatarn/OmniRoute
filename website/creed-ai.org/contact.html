Why Montreal?

Montreal is home to Mila, CIFAR's Pan-Canadian AI Strategy, OBVIA, and the Montreal Declaration on Responsible AI. We are part of the world's densest ecosystem for responsible AI research and governance — the right place to build enforceable ethics for artificial intelligence.
