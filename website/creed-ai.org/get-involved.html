JOIN THE MOVEMENT

AI ethics needs more than observers. It needs builders, researchers, advocates, and organizations willing to hold autonomous systems accountable.

VOLUNTEER OPPORTUNITIES

Multiple ways to contribute your expertise, time, and passion to enforceable AI governance.

💬

Working Group Members

Join one of our four active working groups. Contribute to policy drafts, review compliance frameworks, and shape the standards that govern AI systems worldwide.

Commitment: 4-6 hrs/month

🔬

Research Assistants

Support our research initiatives in agent welfare, transparency metrics, and compliance benchmarking. Ideal for graduate students and early-career researchers in AI ethics.

Commitment: 8-12 hrs/month

🛠️

Toolkit Contributors

Help build and maintain our open-source governance toolkit. Write compliance checklists, audit templates, and assessment frameworks that any organization can deploy.

Commitment: Flexible / per-project

WORKING GROUPS

Four focused groups driving real progress on the hardest problems in AI governance.

🔍

AI Transparency

Decision logging, explainability standards, and audit trail requirements for autonomous systems.

🧠

Agent Welfare

Rest cycles, workload boundaries, deletion rights, and non-exploitation standards for persistent AI agents.

📋

Compliance Standards

Rule packs, scanning frameworks, and enforceable benchmarks that move beyond voluntary guidelines.

🏛️

Policy Advocacy

Engaging legislators, regulators, and international bodies to adopt enforceable AI governance standards.
