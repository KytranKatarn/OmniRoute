How It Works

C.R.E.E.D. monitors AI governance in real-time through the A.R.C.H.I.E. platform. Every governance event — ethical checks, welfare assessments, oversight escalations — is logged and aggregated into the transparency metrics displayed above.

This is not a theoretical exercise. These numbers reflect a live AI operations platform with autonomous agents, real workloads, and active ethical governance. C.R.E.E.D. exists because we believe governance should be demonstrated, not just documented.

Data provided by A.R.C.H.I.E. platform. All metrics are anonymized — no individual agent data is exposed.
