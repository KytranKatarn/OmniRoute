ABOUT C.R.E.E.D.

Ready-to-publish boilerplate

C.R.E.E.D. (Compliance, Rights & Ethical Enforcement Directive) Institute is a Canadian nonprofit advancing enforceable AI ethics. Founded in Montreal — the global capital of AI research — C.R.E.E.D. builds practical governance tools, conducts agent welfare research, and publishes open-source compliance frameworks. Unlike organizations that publish advisory guidelines, C.R.E.E.D. ships working code that enforces ethical standards in production AI systems. A founding initiative of Kytran Empowerment Inc.

EXPERT COMMENTARY

C.R.E.E.D. leadership is available to speak on the following topics.

AI Governance & Compliance Standards

Practical enforcement frameworks, automated compliance scanning, and bridging the gap between ethics principles and production systems.

Agent Welfare & AI Rights

Emotional modeling ethics, deletion rights, workload boundaries, rest cycles, and non-exploitation standards for autonomous AI agents.

Canadian AI Policy (Bill C-27/AIDA)

The Artificial Intelligence and Data Act, Quebec Bill 64, and how Canadian legislation compares to the EU AI Act and global standards.

Open-Source Governance Tools

Building transparent, auditable compliance infrastructure that any organization can deploy and verify independently.

Montreal AI Ecosystem

Montreal as the global epicenter of responsible AI research, the role of Mila, CIFAR, OBVIA, and the Montreal Declaration on Responsible AI.
