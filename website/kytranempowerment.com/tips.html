Platform Tips

Get more out of your A.R.C.H.I.E. subscription with these practical tips.

Use Department HQ for Agent Tasks

All agent assignments flow through Department HQ. Dispatch tasks to the right department and let the agents handle execution autonomously.

Check the Knowledge Base

The platform maintains 25,700+ knowledge entries. Search before asking an agent to build something — the answer may already exist.

Monitor with C.R.E.E.D.

Every agent action is governed by the C.R.E.E.D. ethics layer. Review the governance audit log to see every decision and its rationale.

AI Best Practices

Lessons from running a 50+ agent, 17-department AI platform in production.

Search Before Building

Always query your knowledge base before asking an AI to create something from scratch. Past decisions, patterns, and mistakes are all searchable.

Two Strikes and Escalate

If an agent fails twice on the same task, escalate to a human. Don't let AI spiral through endless retries — know when to take over.

Verify, Don't Trust

Never accept "done" without live proof. Every completed task should have a verifiable output — a screenshot, a log entry, a measurable result.
