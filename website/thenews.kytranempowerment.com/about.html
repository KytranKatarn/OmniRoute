GLOBAL INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD

Kytran News is a real-time global intelligence monitoring platform that aggregates data from multiple open sources to provide a comprehensive view of world events as they unfold.

What We Track

  • Armed Conflicts & Wars — Active conflict zones, military operations, casualties
  • Natural Disasters — Earthquakes, wildfires, volcanic eruptions, floods, storms
  • Weather Alerts — Severe weather warnings, extreme heat/cold advisories
  • Cyber Threats — DDoS attacks, ransomware campaigns, state-sponsored hacking
  • Protests & Civil Unrest — Demonstrations, riots, political instability
  • Space Weather — Solar flares, geomagnetic storms, radiation events
  • Nuclear Radiation — Monitoring station readings worldwide
  • Disease Outbreaks — Epidemics, pandemics, public health emergencies
  • Economic Indicators — Market disruptions, financial center status
  • Sanctions — International sanctions regimes by country
  • Military & Nuclear Sites — Known installations worldwide
  • Internet Outages — BGP disruptions, submarine cable cuts, censorship
  • Live Air Traffic — Real-time aircraft positions
  • Maritime Traffic — Major shipping routes and port activity
  • Air Quality — AQI readings in major cities
  • Social Trends — Viral topics and sentiment by region
  • Terrorism Incidents — Attacks, threats, and security events
  • Power Outages — Reported blackouts and infrastructure failures

Data Sources

GDELT Project
Global event monitoring, news, conflict data
USGS
Earthquake data worldwide
NASA EONET

NWS (NOAA)

NOAA SWPC

ReliefWeb

OFAC / EU / UN
International sanctions data
IAEA Network
Radiation monitoring stations

Technology

Built with deck.gl for WebGL-accelerated map rendering, MapLibre GL JS for base map tiles (CARTO Dark Matter), and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build tools — just fast, lean code.
