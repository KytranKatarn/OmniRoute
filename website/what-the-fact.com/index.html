The Process

From Raw Story to Verified Truth

Five specialized AI agents process every story through a transparent, auditable pipeline — no shortcuts, no black boxes.

01

I.N.K.

Investigative News & Knowledge

Scours thousands of sources in real time — wire services, local papers, primary documents — to gather the raw facts of a story.

02

B.I.A.S.

Balanced Information Analysis & Scoring

Scores every source on the political spectrum, flags loaded language, and maps how the same story is being told differently across the media landscape.

03

V.E.R.I.F.Y.

Verification, Evidence Review & Integrity

Cross-references every claim against primary sources, government data, and academic research. Assigns confidence ratings with linked evidence.

04

D.R.A.F.T.

Data Reduction & Factual Translation

Distills verified information into clear, balanced reporting. Language is neutral, sourcing is explicit, and every editorial choice is logged.

05

P.U.B.L.I.S.H.

Platform Upload, Broadcast & Live Integration

Delivers the final article with full audit trail attached — readers can inspect every source, every bias score, and every AI decision.

Every pipeline run is logged and audited by C.R.E.E.D. — our open-source AI governance platform.
