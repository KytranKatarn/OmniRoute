How a blind spot is identified

Every article in a cluster is banded by its own bias score (left, center-left, center, center-right, right). A side is called silent when the quiet side holds a quarter or less of the two-sided (left + right) coverage and is at least three articles behind, out of at least four articles total. This measures which side of the spectrum covered a story, not how many outlets in total ran it — see the archive for the outlet-count view.
