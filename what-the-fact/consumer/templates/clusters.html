How a cluster's lean is reported

The median lean is the middle band across the cluster, not an average. Averaging a bimodal cluster — half Left, half Right — would report a centre that nobody actually published. The spread line names the two ends of the range, so a tight cluster and a split one can never read the same. Bands are scored per article, not fixed to an outlet: the same newsroom can land in different bands on different stories.
