Read this page carefully

A match across registers is a fact about paperwork, not a finding about a person. Two records sharing a name means both records exist. It does not mean they are connected, and it does not imply wrongdoing in either.

There is no score and no ranking here. Entities are ordered by how many registers they appear in, which is a count of records rather than a measure of influence or of conduct.

Names are matched by exact text. Common names will produce false matches in any system that does this, which is why every entity page names its specific records and their source register rather than the name alone.
