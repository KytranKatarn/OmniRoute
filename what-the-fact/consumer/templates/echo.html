What this page will not say

It will not say a slogan is effective, honest or good — it reports how often the phrase appears and by whom it is commonly used. A phrase repeated by many outlets may be quotable, may be false and need rebutting, or may just be what happened that day; this page cannot tell those apart. It also cannot tell you who first said a phrase repeated it verbatim versus paraphrased it — a text match either found the phrase or it didn't.
