How this is measured

Average miss is the mean absolute difference between the forecast high and the observed high — how far off, ignoring direction. Direction of error is the mean signed difference — whether the forecast leans cold or warm. Both are shown because they answer different questions: a large miss with no direction is noise, while a small miss with a steady direction is a correctable systematic error. A day we did not observe produces no comparison at all; it is never scored as a perfect forecast.

Our record begins {% if s.window_start %}{{ s.window_start.strftime('%-d %B %Y') }}{% endif %} and covers {{ s.window_days }} days. That is enough to see a bias, not enough to call it seasonal — the window is stated here rather than left for the reader to assume, and it deepens by a day every day.
