About GIC appointments
The Governor General appoints individuals to federal positions on the advice of Cabinet — these are Orders-in-Council (OICs). They cover federal judges, Crown corporation executives, regulatory board members (CRTC, CIRB, IRB), deputy ministers, and hundreds of federal tribunal members. Every appointment is an exercise of executive patronage. Data sourced from the Privy Council OIC database ↗.
