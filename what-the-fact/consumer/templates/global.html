Bilateral is aid Canada directs to a country or project. Multilateral is shown as total minus bilateral — money pooled in bodies such as the World Bank or UN agencies, where a Canadian dollar cannot be traced to a project. It is derived here, not an OECD line. Only years where the OECD publishes the total, the bilateral part and the share of GNI together are shown (2018 onward); earlier years use a different method and are not spliced on.
