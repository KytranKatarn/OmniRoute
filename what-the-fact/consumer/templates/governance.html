Badge colour is set by C.R.E.E.D. from the score, not by us: green at 90% and above, blue at 80%, yellow at 70%, red below 60%. The scan runs every six hours against 178 compliance rules in five rule packs; every result is public through a no-auth API, which is how a reader can check the figure on this page against the source.

How this newsroom participates
Every action emits an event
Bias scores, verdicts, summaries and publishes each send a governance event to C.R.E.E.D., where it is scored against the rule packs. Nothing is sampled.
Every agent publishes a model card
B.I.A.S., V.E.R.I.F.Y., D.R.A.F.T. and the rest disclose their model, prompt and known limitations, so a reader sees which system shaped a story and how it can fail.
Method is disclosed, not just results
Lean scoring follows a published methodology with outlet-level and article-level breakdowns, so the public can challenge the scoring itself — the standard C.R.E.E.D. asks of every monitored system.
The grade is theirs, and it is public
C.R.E.E.D. scores the what-the-fact organization; we display the result. A failing pillar stays on the page until the events say otherwise.
