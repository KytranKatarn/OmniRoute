Why Officers of Parliament matter

Unlike ministers or departments, Officers of Parliament serve fixed terms and cannot be removed by the Prime Minister. They report directly to the House of Commons or Senate — creating a firewall between their findings and political interference. When the Auditor General flags waste, or the Ethics Commissioner rules on a conflict of interest, it becomes a formal parliamentary record.
