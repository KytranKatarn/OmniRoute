Votes against seats

The gap column is seat share minus vote share, in percentage points. A positive number means the party holds a larger share of the House than of the vote. It is arithmetic about the electoral system, not a judgement of the party.

What each party’s MPs cost taxpayers

House of Commons members’ expenses (salaries of their staff, travel, hospitality and contracts), charged to the party the MP sat for in that quarter — not the party they sit for today. A cost incurred after an MP left office (office wind-down) is charged to the party they last sat for, and retired MPs are included. Complete calendar years only. Totals follow seat counts, so the last column gives the cost per MP in {{ party_costs.latest }}, and “Retired” is the share of each party’s total incurred by MPs no longer sitting.
