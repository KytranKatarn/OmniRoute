About the overnight rate
The Bank of Canada sets the target for the overnight rate — the interest rate at which major financial institutions borrow and lend one-day funds. Changes ripple through prime rates, mortgages, and the Canadian dollar. The BoC announces rate decisions 8 times per year at fixed dates. Data sourced from the Bank of Canada Valet API (series V122514).
