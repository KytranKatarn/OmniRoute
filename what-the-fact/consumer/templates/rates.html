Bank of Canada rate decisions

Every cut, hike and hold since 2000 · Bank of Canada Valet API (series V122514)

The overnight rate is one of the most closely watched numbers in Canadian economic news — it sets the base cost of borrowing that ripples into mortgages, lines of credit and the loonie, and it moves (or holds) on eight fixed, pre-announced dates a year, so a rate story can always be checked against the decision it describes.

About the overnight rate

The Bank of Canada sets the target for the overnight rate — the interest rate at which major financial institutions borrow and lend one-day funds. Changes ripple through prime rates, mortgages and the Canadian dollar. The BoC announces rate decisions 8 times per year at fixed dates.
