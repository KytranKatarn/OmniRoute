Forecast accuracy

Every forecast this site publishes is kept and compared with the observation that followed. The measure is mean absolute error in degrees Celsius, and the bar to clear is persistence — the naive forecast that tomorrow will be like today. A model that cannot beat persistence is not adding information.

How this is scored
Each forecast is frozen at issue time and compared with the MSC observation for the same station and hour. No forecast is revised after the fact, none are dropped for being embarrassing, and the persistence baseline comes from the same observation series.
