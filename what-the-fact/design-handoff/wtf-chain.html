Reading a chain honestly

A link is a record that two entities are connected in a public dataset — a donation filed, a contract awarded, a vote cast, a story published. It is not a finding that one caused the other. Dashed links are positions in the chain where no dataset has linked in yet; they are shown rather than hidden so the shape of what we cannot see is as visible as what we can.
