About CRTC decisions

The CRTC regulates Canada’s broadcasting system (TV, radio, streaming) and telecommunications (internet, wireless, phone). Its decisions affect every media outlet, telecom provider and online platform operating in Canada. A linked source marks a ruling whose subject matches a WTF-tracked news outlet.
