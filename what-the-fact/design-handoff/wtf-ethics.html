Ethics and integrity commissioners

Ethics rulings

Every conflict-of-interest and integrity finding on record, with the person it concerns named and the report cited. A finding is about a rule, not a verdict on a career.

What a contravention finding is, and is not

A contravention means a specific section of a conflict-of-interest act or members’ code was breached. It is not a criminal finding. These commissioners can investigate, find and publish. They cannot fine, sanction or remove anyone — at most they recommend a sanction to the legislature, which may ignore it.

The most common outcome of an ethics investigation is no contravention, and that is a real result rather than a technicality. Those reports are far less covered than adverse findings, which is a coverage bias this page cannot correct but can name.
