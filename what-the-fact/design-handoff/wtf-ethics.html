Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner of Canada

Ethics Commissioner reports

Investigation reports on MPs, ministers and public office holders since 2015. A report is a finding about a rule, not a verdict on a person — most conclude that no contravention occurred, and those are published here too.

Reading a finding correctly

“No contravention” is the most common outcome and it is a real result, not a technicality. A contravention finding means a specific section of the Conflict of Interest Act or the Members’ Code was breached; it is not a criminal finding and carries no penalty beyond the report itself. The Commissioner cannot fine or remove anyone.
