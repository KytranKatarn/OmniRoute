Vacant posts — the appointments not made

An appointment is a decision. A vacancy is also a decision, taken by not deciding — and it is usually the more consequential one, because a tribunal short of members cannot sit and a board below complement cannot govern. Unlike a by-election, no statute sets a deadline for filling these.

What an Order-in-Council is

A Governor in Council appointment is made by Cabinet, not by a competition or a vote. It is how judges, Crown corporation executives, tribunal members and regulatory boards are filled. Every one is published as a numbered Order-in-Council, which is why the pipeline is auditable even though the decision is discretionary. An appointment appearing here is a public record, not an allegation of patronage.
