What the registry does and does not say

Registration is a legal requirement, so an organisation that files diligently appears more often than one that does not. A high count means visible compliance, not wrongdoing, and absence from this list is not evidence of restraint.

The registry records that a communication took place; it does not publish what was discussed. Anything beyond that — motive, outcome, influence — is inference, not record.

Source: Office of the Commissioner of Lobbying of Canada — Monthly Communication Reports · lobbycanada.gc.ca
