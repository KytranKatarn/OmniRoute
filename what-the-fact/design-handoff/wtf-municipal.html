Why municipal coverage is thin

Council meetings run long, produce no press release, and generate stories only when something goes wrong. Newsroom cuts hit local desks first. The result is that a rezoning affecting thousands of residents is often reported by nobody, while a federal statement affecting no one immediately is reported by everybody.

Municipal government also spends the money closest to people and is revisited least once a decision is made, which is what makes a local blindspot more consequential than a national one.
