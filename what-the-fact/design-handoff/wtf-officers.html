Why Officers of Parliament matter

These offices hold fixed terms and cannot be removed by the Prime Minister. They report directly to the House or the Senate, which creates a firewall against political interference that ordinary departments do not have.

When the Auditor General flags waste, or the Ethics Commissioner rules on a conflict, it becomes a formal parliamentary record — not a press release a government can decline to answer. That is the whole reason the offices exist, and the reason a finding here often outlives the minister it concerns.
