About the overnight rate

The Bank of Canada sets the target for the overnight rate — the interest rate at which major financial institutions borrow and lend one-day funds. Changes ripple through prime rates, mortgages and the Canadian dollar. The Bank announces rate decisions eight times a year at fixed dates. It is not the mortgage rate, and it is not set by the government; a hold is a decision, not an absence of one.
