Score an article

Paste any Canadian news URL and the same six agents that score our feed will score it: lean band, claims found, verdicts where the evidence exists, and how the rest of the press covered the same story.

Article URL

Two free scores a day · unlimited on Starter

What comes back
B.I.A.S.
A lean band for this piece, scored against how everyone else wrote the same story — never a standing label on the outlet.
V.E.R.I.F.Y.
Checkable claims extracted, with a verdict only where a primary document supports one. No verdict is not a pass.
I.N.K.
The cluster: every other outlet that carried this story, and the spread of their framing.
L.O.C.A.L.
Where the story is about, which is often not where it was filed from.
What this will not do

It will not tell you whether an article is true. It reports where the framing sits relative to the rest of the coverage, and which claims can be checked against a document. An article can be scored Centre and still be wrong, and a Left or Right band is a description of language, not an accusation.
